# Individual Reflection Outline

This is deliberately an outline with questions and no text to copy. The reflection is graded individually, must be
about you, and is submitted separately (one page).

**Header:** your name, course and section, project title, date.

## 1. My contribution (about half a page)

- What did you personally do, and when? (For example: decisions, requests, reading code, running tests, finding
  problems, correcting documents, preparing or presenting the demo.) List only what happened.
- Which parts did AI produce that you worked with? Say so. The project README discloses that Claude generated most
  of the code and documents, so your reflection must agree with it.
- How did you check that something you did not write was correct?

## 2. The textbook concept I found most challenging to apply (about half a page)

- Which one concept, and in which section of Coulouris et al.?
- What did you not understand at first?
- Where does it appear in this system? Point to something you have looked at or run yourself.
- What do you understand now, and what is still unclear to you?
